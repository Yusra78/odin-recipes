Lasagna

Home

Description

Lasagna is a popular Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese.

It is baked in the oven until the layers are hot and the cheese is melted.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • 500 g ground beef
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a pan and cook the chopped onion.
  2. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  3. Add the tomato sauce, salt, and black pepper. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Place a layer of sauce in a baking dish, followed by lasagna sheets and cheese.
  5. Repeat the layers until the ingredients are used.
  6. Bake at 180°C (350°F) until the pasta is tender and the cheese is melted, following the pasta package directions.
  7. Allow the lasagna to rest briefly before serving.
