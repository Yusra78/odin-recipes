Pizza

Home

Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish made with dough, tomato sauce, and cheese.

It can be topped with vegetables, meat, and herbs before baking.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 onion, sliced
  • 1/2 bell pepper, sliced
  • Oregano to taste
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven according to the pizza dough instructions.
  2. Roll out the dough on a clean surface.
  3. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Add the mozzarella cheese, onion, and bell pepper.
  5. Sprinkle oregano over the toppings.
  6. Bake until the crust is golden and the cheese has melted, following the dough instructions.
  7. Let the pizza cool slightly, slice, and serve.
