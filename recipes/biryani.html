Chicken Biryani

Home

Description

Chicken biryani is a popular South Asian rice dish made with chicken, rice, and aromatic spices.

It is known for its rich flavor and fragrant aroma.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500 g chicken pieces
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • Biryani spices to taste
  • Fresh coriander and mint
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed

Steps

  1. Wash the rice and soak it for about 20 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot and cook the sliced onions until golden.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and chicken. Cook until the chicken changes color.
  4. Add tomatoes, yogurt, biryani spices, and salt. Cook until the chicken is nearly tender.
  5. Boil the rice separately until it is partially cooked, then drain it.
  6. Layer the rice over the chicken mixture and add the coriander and mint.
  7. Cover the pot and cook on low heat until the chicken is fully cooked and the rice is tender.
  8. Gently mix the layers and serve hot.
